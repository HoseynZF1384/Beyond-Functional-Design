# Beyond Functional Design: The Importance of Unprogrammed Space

**A Conceptual Research Project**

**Author:** Hossein Zoghi Fard

## Overview

This project explores the conceptual importance of unprogrammed space in small-scale environments, examining its relationship with functional design, spatial flexibility, and adaptability.

It questions the limitations of exclusively function-oriented spatial organization and considers the potential value of spaces that allow diverse, spontaneous, and less predetermined uses.

## Research Focus

- Functional design and spatial organization
- Unprogrammed space
- Spatial flexibility and adaptability
- Small-scale environments
- Conceptual approaches to environmental design
